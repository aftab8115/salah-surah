About Salah Surah

Browse short surahs with Arabic, transliteration, and English meaning. Open a surah for verse-by-verse practice and audio from two reciters. Search and filters help you find what to learn next.

Qur’anic text, transliteration, and Saheeh International translation are from AlQuran Cloud. Recitation audio is streamed from EveryAyah. Asma ul Husna is shown as a common learning list; English renderings of the Names are brief meanings.

Dua navigation icon: Tabler Icons “Pray”, MIT license.

Use a trusted mushaf and teacher to verify recitation. This app is a learning aid, not a religious ruling.
